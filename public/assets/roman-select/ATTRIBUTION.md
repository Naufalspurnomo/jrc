# Roman Selection Image Attribution

The six character-selection plates are locally processed duotone derivatives of classical sculpture photographs sourced from Wikimedia Commons. Each image remains a complete museum photograph; no figure anatomy was generated or altered.

## Arena Background

`/assets/arena-pula-wide.webp` and `/assets/arena-pula-mobile.webp` are locally cropped and color-graded derivatives of **"Anfiteatro de Pula, Croacia, 2017-04-16, DD 01-06 PAN"** by Diego Delso.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anfiteatro_de_Pula,_Croacia,_2017-04-16,_DD_01-06_PAN.jpg
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0
- License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0
- Local processing: responsive crops, resize, desaturation, contrast adjustment, brightness adjustment, and restrained warm color grade.
